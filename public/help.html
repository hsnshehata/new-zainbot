Your first conversation

Start with a small test in your own workspace before connecting customer channels.

  1. Create your free account. Register and verify your email if requested, then sign in to your dashboard. Create account
  2. Set up an agent. In AI Agents, add a description or instructions about your business. Add a real question and answer in AI Training so the agent has something to use.
  3. Test and review. Open your agent's website chat link from the dashboard, ask your test question, and check its reply. Adjust your training if it needs work.

The chat demo on the home page is a preview, not your business agent. Shopify and WooCommerce catalogs can be imported manually from Connections using read-only API credentials.
